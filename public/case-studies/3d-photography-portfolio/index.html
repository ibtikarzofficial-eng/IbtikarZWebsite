Interactive 3D Photography Portfolio

A transparent case study of the live IbtikarZ 3D photography portfolio: interaction direction, browser-based 3D, responsive considerations and the technologies behind it.

A live browser-based portfolio built to explore how photography can be presented as an interactive spatial experience instead of a conventional grid alone.

The case study describes the implementation and design direction. It does not claim invented conversion, revenue or engagement results.

Move beyond a normal photography grid.

The concept needed to make a portfolio itself feel like a designed experience while still preserving a conventional content path and usable interaction on different screen sizes.

Make the gallery spatial and responsive to input.

The experience uses browser-native 3D interaction, camera behavior and gallery movement to let the visitor explore images instead of only scrolling past them.

A production-minded 3D web stack.

  • React-based frontend architecture
  • Three.js / React Three Fiber style real-time rendering
  • GLB/GLTF scene assets where required
  • Pointer and scroll interaction
  • Responsive camera and layout behavior
  • Fallback thinking for device constraints

The portfolio is the proof asset.

The strongest evidence is not a screenshot. The live subdomain lets prospective clients test the experience themselves and decide whether the interaction quality and visual direction fit the type of project they want to commission.
