Case study

Picazo’s Flowers: reshaping a local flower website around occasions and enquiry intent.

Responsive website redesign work for Picazo’s Flowers, organized around local ordering, weddings, events and clearer enquiry paths.

Scope

What the work covered

  • Responsive redesign work in Duda
  • Homepage, About and Contact page presentation
  • Weddings, events and order-flowers routes
  • CTA hierarchy and mobile polish
Decisions

What mattered

  • Organize around buying occasions.
  • Give weddings and events enough context to earn trust.
  • Keep ordering and enquiry routes simple on mobile.
  • Connect service content, visual proof and enquiry actions.
