Internal case study

IbtikarZ: rebuilding the site around search intent and lead capture.

A transparent case study of the system behind IbtikarZ: commercial page ownership, preserved search equity, Cloudflare Pages, server-side lead capture, consent-aware analytics and a clear path from search visit to enquiry.

Architecture

What changed

  • Commercial pages now own one clear buyer intent.
  • WordPress, redesign, speed, technical SEO, local SEO and maintenance have dedicated landing pages.
  • Existing 3D URLs with Search Console traction were preserved instead of casually deleted.
  • Old service URLs map to the new owners with permanent redirects.
  • High-intent cost and comparison content supports commercial pages.
Lead system

What happens after the click

  • Free website checkup for visitors who are not ready to buy.
  • Project brief for high-intent visitors.
  • Server-side lead endpoint on Cloudflare Pages Functions.
  • Durable D1 lead storage with optional email/webhook notification.
  • GA4 generate_lead fires only after the backend confirms success.

Why the rebuild was not a URL reset

The aim was to improve the commercial architecture without throwing away the signals Google had already started collecting. URLs with useful search visibility were kept. Thin or redundant service routes were mapped to stronger destination pages with permanent redirects.

Why Cloudflare Pages

The site is static-first for speed and crawlability, while Pages Functions provide server-side lead handling without a traditional application server. D1 can store submissions, and email or CRM forwarding can be added through server-side credentials.

What this case study does not claim

The rebuild does not guarantee rankings, leads or revenue. Search visibility and conversion performance are measured after deployment. The implementation is the evidence; business outcomes need enough post-launch data before they become claims.
