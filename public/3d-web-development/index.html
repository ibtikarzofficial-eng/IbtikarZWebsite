3D Web Development

Premium 3D web development with Three.js, React Three Fiber and WebGL for interactive websites, product experiences, portfolios and browser-based 3D.

When flat screenshots cannot explain the product or the experience, real-time 3D can let visitors rotate, explore, configure or move through it directly in the browser. IbtikarZ builds focused 3D web experiences with a $1,000 minimum engagement.

Final scope and price depend on assets, interactions, integrations, content, platform and delivery requirements.

  • ✓ Scope confirmed before work
  • ✓ Responsive and performance-aware
  • ✓ No guaranteed rankings or revenue claims

Who this is for

Brands, product companies, portfolios, launch campaigns and digital experiences where interaction communicates something that ordinary page sections cannot.

What this solves

3D is often added as decoration. The useful version has a clear job: demonstrate a product, reveal variants, create a memorable portfolio, explain space or support a buying decision.

What the engagement can cover.

  • Three.js and WebGL implementation
  • React Three Fiber when suitable
  • Interactive 3D scenes and camera systems
  • Scroll, pointer and touch interactions
  • GLB/GLTF asset integration
  • Responsive fallback strategy
  • Performance-minded loading
  • SEO-accessible surrounding HTML
  • Analytics events for meaningful interactions

What is scoped separately.

  • Complex 3D modeling unless scoped
  • AR/VR hardware applications by default
  • Unlimited scenes, assets or animation
  • Guaranteed engagement, ranking or sales outcomes

Experience the work rather than watching a mockup

The current flagship IbtikarZ 3D portfolio is a live browser experience at portfolio.ibtikarz.com ↗ . It demonstrates real-time layout, interaction and camera behavior directly in the browser.

3D should be a progressive layer, not a crawlability trap

Important copy, headings, navigation and internal links remain available as HTML. Heavy assets can be compressed, deferred and adapted so the experience does not depend on every device rendering the same scene at full quality.
