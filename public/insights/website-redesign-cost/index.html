How Much Does a Website Redesign Cost?

A practical guide to website redesign cost, including when a focused rescue sprint is enough and when a full rebuild makes more sense.

Updated 24 Sep 2026 • By Muhammad Abdullah Shah

There is no useful single price

A redesign can mean changing typography on a homepage or rebuilding information architecture, templates, forms, analytics and redirects. Those are different projects and should not be quoted as if they were the same.

The biggest cost drivers

Number of templates, quality of existing content, migration complexity, integrations, ecommerce, custom functionality, copywriting needs, accessibility issues and whether URLs must change all affect the scope.

Rescue sprint vs full rebuild

A rescue sprint is useful when the platform and content are fundamentally workable but the homepage, mobile experience, trust, speed or CTA hierarchy are weak. IbtikarZ Rescue Sprints start from PKR 45,000 / $350.

When to rebuild

A rebuild is more justified when the CMS is unmaintainable, the page architecture is fundamentally wrong, the site has accumulated serious technical debt or the business has outgrown the original structure.

Protect SEO during redesign

Inventory indexed URLs, map redirects, preserve pages with existing search traction, verify canonicals and sitemaps, and monitor Search Console after launch. A visual redesign should not casually reset useful URLs.

How to get a useful quote

Provide the current URL, business goal, pages that matter, required integrations and any deadline. A short audit or checkup can identify whether the smallest sensible solution is a sprint or a rebuild.
