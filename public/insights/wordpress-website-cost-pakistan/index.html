How Much Does a WordPress Website Cost in Pakistan?

What does a professional WordPress website cost in Pakistan? See the factors that change price, realistic project scopes and what should be included.

Updated 24 Sep 2026 • By Muhammad Abdullah Shah

Short answer

A basic brochure site can be inexpensive, but a professional lead-generation WordPress website costs more because strategy, content structure, responsive development, forms, performance, analytics and technical SEO all require time. IbtikarZ lead-generation business websites currently start from PKR 70,000, while smaller landing pages and focused fixes start lower.

What changes the price

Page count is only one factor. Custom design, copy support, calculators or eligibility tools, CRM integrations, ecommerce, multilingual content, migration work and the quality of existing assets can change the scope significantly.

What a professional build should include

A serious business build should include responsive implementation, maintainable editing, lead forms, analytics, Search Console, technical SEO foundations, performance checks, launch QA and a handover. Hosting, premium licenses and complex integrations should be stated separately.

When a cheaper site is enough

If the business only needs one clear campaign or service page, a landing page may be more responsible than a five-to-eight-page build. If an existing site is structurally sound, a rescue sprint may create more value than replacing everything.

How to compare quotes

Compare scope, not only price. Ask who is writing or structuring content, how forms are tested, what happens to old URLs, who owns the site, what is included after launch and whether analytics measures successful leads.

IbtikarZ starting points

Landing pages start from PKR 25,000, Website Rescue Sprints from PKR 45,000 and lead-generation business websites from PKR 70,000. Final scope depends on the work actually required.
