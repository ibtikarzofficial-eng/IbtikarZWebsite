Privacy

IbtikarZ collects only the information needed to respond to enquiries, understand website usage with consent and operate the site.

Enquiry data

When you submit a form, IbtikarZ may store your name, email, website URL, project details, service interest, attribution information and submission metadata. This information is used to respond to your request and manage the project opportunity.

Analytics

GA4 and Microsoft Clarity are loaded only after analytics consent. Advertising storage remains disabled. You can decline analytics and still use the website and forms.

Cloudflare

The website is designed for Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process standard request information as part of providing hosting, security and serverless functions.

Lead storage and email

Lead submissions can be stored in Cloudflare D1 and optionally forwarded by email through a configured transactional-email provider. Credentials are kept server-side, not in browser code.
